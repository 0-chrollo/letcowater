Letco Water Solutions Borehole FAQs

How much does borehole drilling cost?

Drilling starts from R350 per metre. The final cost depends on how many metres you would need, the depth and the geological formation, so call or WhatsApp us on 078 378 2121 for a quote.

Can you drill where space is limited?

Yes. For sites with limited space, a small rig is available for tight access.

What is included in a borehole pump package?

We supply and install borehole pumps. Each package is complete and includes a pump, motor, control box, cable, rope, base plate and two adaptors.

Do you install solar borehole pumping systems?

Yes. We provide solar borehole pumping systems, as well as backup water systems.

Do you install home water purification?

Yes. We install home water purification systems designed to provide clean and safe drinking water, remove impurities and germs, and improve taste and odour.

Do you do plumbing and irrigation?

Yes. We carry out plumbing work, install pumps and tank connections at water storage tanks, and provide irrigation services.

Which areas do you work in?

We serve clients across Gauteng, North West and Mpumalanga, including Moloto, KwaNdebele, Witbank and surrounding areas.
